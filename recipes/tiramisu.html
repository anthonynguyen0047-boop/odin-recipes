Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian casserole made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of rich meat or tomato sauce, vegetables, and various cheeses like ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. It is baked until bubbly and browned, resulting in a hearty, comforting, and savory layered dish.

Ingredients

  • Ground meat
  • Tomatoes
  • Herbs & Spices
  • Water and Red Wine

Steps

  1. Brown the meat
  2. Simmer the sauce
  3. Mix the cheese
  4. Cook the pasta
