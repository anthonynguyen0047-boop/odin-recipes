Description

A pizza is a world-famous savory Italian dish consisting of a flattened, round base of leavened wheat dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and various other ingredients. It is traditionally baked at a highly intense temperature in a wood-fired oven and sliced into wedges for serving.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Tomatoes
  • Herbs & Spices
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Stretch the dough
  2. Apply the sauce
  3. Put spices and chesse on top
  4. Cook the pizza
